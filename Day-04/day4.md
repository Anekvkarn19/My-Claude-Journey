# Day 4 — Chain-of-Thought Prompting & Career Roadmap

## 🎯 Objective

The goal of Day 4 was to explore how a structured prompting approach can be used to generate a personalized career roadmap.

I used the provided Chain-of-Thought Prompting template in Claude and answered the four questions about my current situation, skills, target goal, and timeline.

---

## 🧩 Prompt Used

The provided prompt instructed Claude to act as an:

> "Elite AI Career Strategist"

Claude was asked to first collect four pieces of information:

1. Current situation
2. Current skills
3. Target goal
4. Target timeline

After collecting the information, Claude generated a structured one-page AI Engineer career roadmap.

## 🔍 My Biggest Insight

The biggest insight from this experiment was:

> **An AI-generated roadmap is only as personalized and accurate as the context provided to the AI.**

Claude produced a well-structured AI Engineer roadmap, but it underestimated some of my existing experience and suggested starting with areas I already have some exposure to.

This showed me that effective prompting is not only about asking a good question. It is also about providing **accurate, complete and relevant context**.

---

## 🧠 What I Learned

### 1. Structured prompts produce structured outputs

Providing Claude with a defined role, questions, goals and output format resulted in a highly organized roadmap.

### 2. Context matters

The quality of a personalized recommendation depends heavily on the information provided to the AI.

### 3. AI recommendations should be reviewed

An AI-generated roadmap should not automatically be treated as a perfect plan. It needs to be reviewed against my actual skills, goals and experience.

### 4. AI can be used as a planning partner

Claude can help identify possible skill gaps, learning priorities, projects and milestones, making it useful for career planning.

---

## ⚡ Final Reflection

This experiment changed the way I think about AI-generated career advice.

Instead of simply asking AI to create a plan and following it blindly, I can use AI to generate a starting point, critically evaluate the recommendations, and then adapt the plan according to my actual situation.

**Day 4/60 — Learning to use AI as a career planning partner, not as a replacement for my own judgment.**
