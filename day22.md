# Day 22 — Startup Validation: NIRIKSHAK AI

## Startup Discovery Answers

### 1. Startup Idea
NIRIKSHAK AI is an AI/ML, OCR, and computer-vision system designed to assist Legal Metrology inspectors and regulatory authorities in India in verifying compliance of packaged commodities. It checks declarations such as MRP, net quantity, manufacturer details, and country of origin against applicable Legal Metrology requirements.

### 2. Problem Being Solved
The current inspection process relies heavily on manual visual checking of packaged-product labels. This can be slow and inconsistent, while regulatory requirements are becoming more complex. NIRIKSHAK AI aims to assist inspectors with faster, standardized label verification and a digital evidence trail.

### 3. Target Customers
Primary: State Legal Metrology Directorates / District Consumer Affairs offices and their field inspectors.

Secondary / potential B2B segment: Mid-size FMCG, cosmetics, D2C packaged-goods brands, and importers with compliance/QA teams.

### 4. Why I Want to Build It
The project originated as a Smart India Hackathon 2026 prototype around the Legal Metrology inspection problem. The current focus is to validate whether the technology solves a sufficiently painful real-world problem and whether either government inspectors or B2B compliance teams would adopt and pay for it.

### 5. Existing Validation
NIRIKSHAK AI is currently prototype / pre-validation. It is pre-revenue and pre-pilot, with no customer interviews, paying customers, or LOIs yet. The problem has been researched and supported by regulatory/legal evidence, but direct user-level validation is still required.

### 6. Target Market / Country
India, initially focusing on the Legal Metrology and regulatory-compliance ecosystem.

### 7. Startup, Business, or Side Project?
The current intent is to explore NIRIKSHAK AI as a potential startup rather than treating it only as a hackathon submission. The immediate focus is a 30–90 day validation sprint before committing to further engineering. The report recommends testing government and faster-moving B2B opportunities in parallel.

## Key Validation Findings

- Overall recommendation: CONDITIONAL GO
- Founder-Market Fit: 5.8/10
- Technical Skill Fit: 8/10
- Commitment & Grit: 8/10
- Network & Distribution: 3/10
- Financial Runway: 4/10
- Main validation gap: direct conversations with real inspectors and compliance leads.
- Recommended next step: conduct 10–15 structured interviews before investing heavily in further development.
- A potential B2B self-compliance SaaS route may validate faster than a government-only route because government procurement cycles are much longer.
- The report identifies a whitespace around regulator-facing, field-mobile, India-specific compliance inspection tooling.

## Go / No-Go
CONDITIONAL GO

The report recommends proceeding, but treating the next 30–90 days as a validation sprint rather than a build sprint. The biggest unresolved question is whether the intended buyers will actually adopt and pay for the solution.

## 30-Day Action Plan

### Week 1 — Talk to real users
Interview Legal Metrology inspectors / Assistant Controllers and FMCG/D2C compliance or QA leads.

### Week 2 — Sharpen the wedge
Choose one narrow workflow to validate first, such as checking a label photo against mandatory LMPC declarations.

### Week 3 — Build a testable demo + find a pilot
Create a demo usable by a non-technical inspector or QA person and approach potential pilot users.

### Week 4 — Decide and plan
Compare response rate, pilot willingness, and eventual willingness to pay across government and B2B segments, then choose the next 90-day direction.

## Key Learning

The biggest lesson from this validation exercise was that building a technically feasible prototype is not the same as validating a startup. The next milestone for NIRIKSHAK AI is not more features; it is evidence from real users about workflow, pain, adoption, and willingness to pay.
