# Day 24 — Business Strategy & Investability: NIRIKSHAK AI

## Objective
Today's task evaluated NIRIKSHAK AI from a business and founder perspective using a revenue-first, distribution-first, and evidence-first approach.

## Overall Verdict
**VALIDATE — 46.6 / 100**

The report found a real regulatory problem and a technically workable MVP, but no paying customers, LOIs, or validated willingness-to-pay yet.

## Business Reality Check
- **Primary buyer:** State Legal Metrology departments and field inspectors.
- **Secondary buyer:** FMCG/D2C compliance and QA teams.
- Government is mission-aligned but has a **12–24 month** sales cycle.
- B2B compliance teams may provide a faster-paying near-term revenue path.
- Current acquisition channels remain unproven.
- The weakest assumption is that either buyer will change the existing process without a forcing event or internal champion.

## Business Model
### Value Proposition
Faster, standardized, legally-defensible label-compliance checks using OCR and a rule engine for government users, and self-serve pre-print SKU compliance checks for B2B customers.

### Customers
- State Legal Metrology departments and inspectors
- FMCG/D2C/importer compliance and QA teams

### Channels
SIH/mentor network, GeM Startup Runway, direct department outreach, direct B2B outreach, content, and referrals.

### Revenue Streams
Government pilot/MoU contracts, B2B SaaS subscriptions, and future platform API/white-label licensing.

## Revenue & Pricing
- Government pilot: **₹15–40L per 6–12 month pilot**
- B2B self-serve SaaS: **₹15,000–60,000/year per brand**

These are hypotheses to test through customer discovery, not fixed prices.

## Go-To-Market Strategy
**Phase 1 (0–30 days):** 10–15 discovery interviews with inspectors and compliance leads.

**Phase 2 (30–90 days):** 1–2 government MoU pilots and 2–3 B2B paid/free trials in parallel.

**Phase 3 (90+ days):** Focus on the segment showing real payment intent and use pilot evidence for further acquisition.

## First 100 Users Plan
1. Complete 10–15 discovery interviews.
2. Convert interviews into pilot/trial commitments.
3. Onboard 10–20 real users in Month 2.
4. Use early evidence to approach additional states and brands.
5. Target 100 cumulative users by the end of Month 3.

## Competitive Position & Moat
Potential differentiation:
- Regulator-side focus
- India-specific LMPC rule engine
- Field-mobile workflow
- Technical capability demonstrated through the SIH prototype

These advantages are currently **unproven** and strengthen only with real customer relationships and usage data.

## Reverse SWOT Insights
- No direct competitor could mean either open opportunity or difficult market.
- Weak government network may support a B2B-first GTM.
- Regulatory urgency may not convert into budget.
- Incumbent QA vendors entering the space could create a partnership/acquisition opportunity.

## Key Risks
- Long government sales cycles may exhaust founder runway.
- No paying customers or LOIs yet.
- Solo-founder bandwidth.
- Government data-access and hosting restrictions.
- Existing QA vendors may expand into the regulatory use case.

- Ask about budget authority and willingness to pay.
- Convert the SIH prototype into a single-workflow clickable MVP.
- Approach two states for potential MoU pilots.
- Approach five SME/D2C brands for B2B trials.

## Key Learning
A technically promising product still needs a sustainable business model, reliable distribution, and evidence of willingness to pay.

For NIRIKSHAK AI, the immediate priority is **validation**: talk to real customers, test pricing, secure pilot commitments, and determine which segment shows genuine demand.
