⚡ Biggest anomaly

Kolkata posts the best AQI in the dataset (61 · Satisfactory) yet one of the weakest water-quality scores (30 · Grade D) — clean air and clean water don't move together here.

😮 Most surprising

Bengaluru's air (67 AQI, Satisfactory) looks reassuring, but every tested tap-water sample failed BIS standards on turbidity and coliform — a "clean city" reputation that doesn't extend to the tap.

📌 Recommended actions (network-wide)

  • Treat NCR readings (Ghaziabad, Noida, Meerut, Delhi, Muzaffarnagar) as a single high-exposure cluster — indoor filtration matters most here.
  • Don't assume good AQI means safe tap water, or vice versa — check both scores independently for any city.
  • In "Measured" water-quality cities, still filter/boil — BIS testing found zero fully-compliant metros except Mumbai.
  • Reschedule outdoor exercise to early morning in any city currently in Moderate or worse.

🧭 Trend read-out

The Indo-Gangetic Plain cluster (Ghaziabad, Noida, Meerut, Delhi, Muzaffarnagar) averages roughly 204 AQI — about 2.7× higher than the six southern/western/eastern metros in this dataset, consistent with the region's seasonal dust, construction and crop-residue burning load reported by CPCB.

📝 Executive Summary
11 cities · avg AQI 134 · Aug 2026 snapshot

Across the 11 cities analyzed, average AQI sits at 134 — Moderate, tipping toward Poor. The Indo-Gangetic Plain cluster around Delhi drags the network average up sharply: Ghaziabad posts the worst reading (AQI 272, Poor) and, combined with weaker estimated water quality, ranks as the most polluted city overall — notable since it's also the location this report was generated for. Kolkata has the cleanest air (AQI 61) but middling water. Mumbai comes out as the single best-scoring city overall (88/100, Grade A) on the strength of being the only metro where all tested tap-water samples met BIS drinking-water standards — air quality alone doesn't decide who's "cleanest." The clearest actionable pattern: water quality is uncorrelated with air quality in this dataset, so any personal risk assessment needs to check both, not infer one from the other.

Data & methodology. AQI/PM readings compiled from CPCB's NCR bulletin, IQAir and AQI.in live monitoring (most recent available reading per city, Aug 2026); category bands follow CPCB's official 6-tier scale. Where a city's granular PM2.5/PM10 wasn't published alongside its AQI, values were derived using CPCB's standard AQI sub-index breakpoint formula for internal consistency — flagged in the table below. Water-quality scores are Measured where drawn from the 2019 Bureau of Indian Standards drinking-water compliance study (the most recent nationwide comparative dataset), and Estimated for NCR towns not covered by that study, modeled from regional CGWB groundwater-quality reporting. This tool is educational, not a medical or environmental-testing device — see a doctor for personal health decisions and a certified lab for water testing.
