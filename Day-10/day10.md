---

## Key Learnings

### 1. AI can accelerate frontend development

Claude can convert structured requirements and content into a functional website quickly.

### 2. Good input leads to better output

Providing accurate personal information, skills, projects, and design requirements helped Claude create a more relevant portfolio.

### 3. AI-generated code still needs testing

Even when AI generates the complete application, the developer needs to test the output and check links, responsiveness, layout, and functionality.

---

## Biggest Takeaway

> **AI can significantly speed up development, but the developer still needs to provide direction, review the output, and test the final product.**
